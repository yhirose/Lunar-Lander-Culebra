Controls

  

    
P
start a round

    
← →

↑ ↓
strengthen and weaken the thrusters

    
Q
back to the title screen

  


  
Landing

  

    Put both feet down under 12 units of vertical speed and 25 of horizontal for a good
    landing, worth 50 points and 50 units of fuel back. The flattened pads marked 2x
    through 5x multiply the score, and the narrower the pad the more it pays. Come in
    faster and it is a hard landing or a crash, and a crash costs 100 fuel. When the tank
    empties, the game is over.
